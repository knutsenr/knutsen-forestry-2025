Welcome to Forestry Foundations

The goal of this website is to provide resources for local landowners, residents of Apalachia, and interested parties easier access to relevant material relating to forestry, land use, and local history. Currently the site contains:

  • A collection of relevant articles.
  • Maps and Photography which can be viewed seperately or together.

In the future we hope to provide contact with relevant subject matter experts and other features. Thank you for visiting!
